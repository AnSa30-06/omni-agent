# The Decisions list

**What it does.** Every decision, open or closed, with filters.

**Why it exists.** Today shows you what to do now. This is where you go to find
something specific, review what you dismissed, or check what your team resolved
last month.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Open (the default), New, Accepted, In progress, Waiting, Snoozed, Resolved, Dismissed, Everything |
| **Severity** | Critical, high, medium, low |
| **Kind** | Churn risk, expansion, payment, company-wide |
| **Owner** | Anyone, nobody yet, or a name |
| **Sort** | Most serious, newest, due first |

The filters are in the address, so a filtered view can be bookmarked.

## The statuses

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **New** | Raised, not looked at. |
| **Accepted** | You are taking it on. |
| **In progress** | Work has started. Set automatically when you complete an action. |
| **Waiting** | You have done your part and are waiting on the customer. Nudges after a week. |
| **Snoozed** | Not now. Comes back on a date, or earlier if things get worse. |
| **Resolved** | Finished, with an outcome recorded. |
| **Dismissed** | Not a real problem. Has a reason attached. |

## What to expect

Most of your time is on **Open**. Two other views are worth a look now and then:

- **Dismissed** — what you decided was not worth acting on. If the same kind
  keeps appearing here, a threshold is wrong for your business.
- **Resolved** — what happened, and whether what you did worked.

## Empty state

"Nothing matches" with a button back to open decisions. If it says that on the
Open filter, look at [Today](today.md) and
[Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
